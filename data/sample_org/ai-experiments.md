# Meridian Transit Authority — AI experiments

## Current activity

Two pilots ran in the last year. A demand-forecasting notebook produced results
an analyst considered promising; it was never evaluated against a held-out
period and has not run since its author changed team. A chatbot pilot over the
public timetable was withdrawn after it answered questions about routes that do
not exist.

## Evaluation

Neither pilot defined success criteria before starting. The forecasting
notebook was judged by eye against the previous year. The chatbot had no
groundedness check of any kind, which is how the invented routes reached users.

## Governance

There is no register of AI systems in use, no approval step before a pilot
touches production data, and no named accountable owner for either pilot. The
Authority's procurement rules do mention automated decision-making, requiring a
documented human review step for decisions affecting the public.

## Infrastructure

Pilots ran on analysts' laptops and one unmanaged cloud account paid for on a
departmental card. Costs were not tracked per experiment.
