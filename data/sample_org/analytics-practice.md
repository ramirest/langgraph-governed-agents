# Meridian Transit Authority — Analytics practice

## Team

Six analysts, distributed across Planning, Revenue Operations and the
Director's office. They do not share a backlog, a repository or a definition
review. Two of them maintain overlapping ridership models.

## Definitions

"Ridership" has three definitions in active use: boardings, unique farecard
taps per day, and estimated journeys after transfer inference. Reports do not
state which they use. The Director's monthly pack and the Planning dashboard
disagree by roughly 8% for this reason.

## Tooling

Analysts work in a mix of SQL, spreadsheets and one Python notebook environment
installed by an analyst who has since moved to Planning. Notebooks are not
version-controlled.

## Delivery

Reports are delivered as PDF attachments. There is no self-service layer, so
every question becomes a request, and the analysts estimate that recurring
requests consume about half their capacity.
